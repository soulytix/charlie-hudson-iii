Biography

About

A character actor whose career reads as a body of work rather than a résumé.

Charlie Hudson III is an actor from Montgomery, Alabama, whose work moves between the American regional stage, Off-Broadway, Broadway and prestige television.

He trained at Alabama State University and at the Brown University / Trinity Repertory Consortium, MFA Class of 2007, with festival work at the Williamstown Theatre Festival in 2003 and 2006.

In New York he has appeared at The Public Theater, Signature Theatre and MCC Theater, and was part of the company of the 2014 Broadway revival of A Raisin in the Sun — the production that won the Tony Award for Best Revival of a Play.

On screen his credits include Daredevil: Born Again, Archive 81, The Night Of and The Good Fight. In 2025 he returned to Yale Repertory Theatre as Hodge Bishop in Spunk.
